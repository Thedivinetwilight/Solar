## A.2 Fig. S32 — axis calibration and branch inventory

Calibration (from the 144-dpi native raster, ≥4 ticks per axis):

- x: `col = −109.0 + 0.5845·nm` (max residual 0.2 px = 0.34 nm)
- y: `row = 414.4 − 4.0229·EQE%` (max residual 2.1 px = 0.52 pp)
- Pixel-uncertainty band on integrals: ±0.52 pp (≈ ±0.35 mA/cm² for the Si curve)

Branches extracted (color-segmented, per-color masks):

| branch | color | description |
|---|---|---|
| HZB perovskite EQE | dark red RGB(139,27,67) | certified champion, in-house (HZB), flat front |
| HZB silicon EQE | dark red | ditto |
| HZB 1-R | dark red dashed | reflection (1−R) of the champion cell |
| Sahli perovskite EQE | yellow RGB(208,189,133) | ref-62 fully textured cell (Sahli et al. 2018) |
| Sahli silicon EQE | yellow | ditto |
| Sahli 1-R | yellow dashed | ditto |

Figure labels read from the pixels: HZB 19.41 / 20.18 / cumulative 39.59;
Sahli 20.1 / 20.3 / cumulative 40.4. Caption (verbatim, p55):
"External quantum efficiencies of the certified champion device in this
publication (measured in-house) and of the publication from Sahli et al. (62).
The latter shows that the textured front side can increase the photogenerated
current density by reduced reflection. This led to a cumulative current
density of 40.4 mA cm-2, whereas the flat front side of this publication led
to 39.59 mA cm-2. Additionally, the dashed lines show the reflection (1-R) of
the respective cell."

Structural rules (Objective A.6 crossover-identity): perovskite/Si crossings
located at 733.5 nm (HZB) and 743.0 nm (Sahli); a ±2.5 nm crossing zone merges
the two solid branches (mean of solid clusters only, 1-R excluded); the
perovskite tail and Si are separated by the row/gap rules; the 1-R dashed line
is tracked per color (its envelopes differ by 2–3 pp: the textured yellow 1-R
sits above the flat dark-red 1-R, physically consistent with reduced
reflection). Occlusion is flagged, not silently assigned: the yellow perovskite
curve is hidden under the dark-red line in ≈76 nm of windows (515–528, 657–720).

## A.3 Three-method digitization results (Fig. S32, final)

Three independent methods, one per rendering route (no shared state):
**M1** native-pixel color segmentation of the 144-dpi raster; **M2** color
segmentation of an independent 200-dpi re-render; **M3** semi-manual
zoom reading (36 perov / 72 Si / 30 1-R points). Integration on the
bit-exact recovered spectrum (A.2-cycle-1; fingerprint
`59040a85…d0eb0`), window 300–1250 nm (project convention; 300–1200
identical to 4 decimals; 300–1100 in the sensitivity table). **No vertical
scaling or offset anywhere** (machine-enforced guard).

| method | perovskite (label 19.41) | silicon (label 20.18) | sum (label 39.59) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **M1 native** | **19.4301** (+0.02) | **20.1846** (+0.00) | **39.6146** (+0.02) |
| M2 200-dpi re-render | 19.5654 (+0.16) | 20.1857 (+0.01) | 39.7511 (+0.16) |
| M3 manual zoom | 19.3210 (−0.09) | 20.1073 (−0.07) | 39.4282 (−0.16) |
| spread across methods | 19.321–19.565 | 20.107–20.186 | 39.428–39.751 |
| pixel-uncertainty band (y-calib 0.52 pp) | ±0.35 | ±0.35 | — |

Sahli (comparison device) branches, M1:

| branch | digitized (label) | Δ |
|---|---|---|
| perovskite (20.1) | 19.6044 | −0.49 |
| silicon (20.3) | 19.4531 | −0.85 |
| sum (40.4) | 39.0575 | −1.34 (−3.3%) |

The drawn yellow curves are a **schematic of the ref-62 measured values**:
(i) ≈76 nm of the yellow perovskite curve is occluded by the dark-red line
(hidden windows 515–528 and 657–720), (ii) the low-fidelity yellow rendering
differs by ≤1 pp from the dark-red trace where visible. The caption's
"cumulative current density of 40.4" is the *measured* value from ref 62; the
drawn curves agree with it within 3.3%. This is documented, not claimed as a
pixel-exact integral.

1-R (reflection) branches, integral over 300–1200 nm:

| branch | 1-R Jph proxy | reflection loss vs 100%-EQE |
|---|---|---|
| HZB (M1) | 42.2229 | 4.2333 mA/cm² |
| HZB (M2) | 44.1192 | 2.3370 |
| Sahli (M1) | 45.1158 | 1.3404 |

The textured (Sahli) 1-R carries ~1.3 mA/cm² less reflection loss than the
flat (HZB) 1-R — the direction and qualitative magnitude stated in the caption
("reduced reflection … increase the photogenerated current density"). (The
Jph difference 40.4−39.59=0.81 is smaller than the 1-R difference because
absorption is <100%; consistency check, not an identity.)

## A.6 Resolution of the 20.18 vs ~16.90–17.11 conflict

**VERDICT: RESOLVED in favor of the source-reported values 19.41 / 20.18 /
39.59**, with the conflict re-classified as
SOURCE-REPORTING_INCONSISTENCY (low-fidelity historical digitization), cause
closed as far as the public record allows:

1. **The source-reported family is independently reproducible from the
   highest-resolution lawful figure.** Three independent digitizations of
   Fig. S32 (M1/M2/M3, §A.3) yield perovskite 19.321–19.565, silicon
   20.107–20.186, sum 39.428–39.751. M1 (native pixels, zero shared state)
   reproduces the printed labels to +0.02 / +0.00 / +0.02 mA/cm² — far inside
   the 0.52-pp pixel-uncertainty band. The champion's plotted Si branch is
   the branch behind 20.18.
2. **The conflicting 16.90–17.11 family is not reproducible from the current
   sources.** Every V1.8 method on both committed figures falls in
   18.71–20.19 (Si); none approaches 16.90–17.11. The values 17.110498
   (V1.5/V1.6, "S32", 300–1250 nm) and 16.901937 (V1.4/V1.6, "Fig 4E") are
   therefore **SUPERSEDED-UNREPRODUCIBLE**: their curve files are
   MISSING-VERIFIED, their extraction choices are not re-inspectable, and
   they are inconsistent with the committed highest-resolution raster. They
   remain in the ledger, immutable, as HANDOFF-QUOTED history
   (`apmt/ledger.py`), and the "required" 1.1793929× rescale derived from
   them remains machine-refused (it was never needed).
3. **Hypothesis adjudication** (cycle-1 set, §old-A.5):
   - H1 (spectrum/window/shift): EXCLUDED (re-anchored on the bit-exact
     spectrum; §A.5).
   - H2 (1-R misread as EQE): EXCLUDED (1-R integrals 42.2–45.1; separate
     dashed curves; §A.3).
   - H3 (plotted Si ≠ dataset behind 20.18): **EXCLUDED** — the plotted S32
     Si branch integrates to 20.107–20.186, i.e. it *is* the 20.18 branch.
   - H4 (typo): EXCLUDED as an explanation — the family is internally exact
     (39.59, 0.77) and now pixel-reproducible.
   - H5 (different measurement context for the plotted curve): **EXCLUDED as
     far as testable** — the SI provides no alternate context and the
     plotted curve matches the label; the in-house (HZB) measurement named in
     the caption is the one plotted.
   - H6 (mask vs full-stack derivation): **EXCLUDED as far as testable** —
     same reasoning; no mask-derivation signature in the pixels.
   The residual irreducible unknowns (exact raw measurement behind the
   in-house EQE; why the V1.4–V1.6 pipeline produced 16.90–17.11) are
   documented as CLOSED-FOR-V1.8: they no longer affect any champion number,
   because the champion numbers are directly reproduced from pixels.
4. **Sahli cross-check** (§A.3) independently confirms the figure's fidelity:
   the second device's drawn curves integrate to within 3.3% of its published
