## A.2 What WAS completed this cycle (no images required)

1. **Primary-text verification of the Fig. 4E statements** (accepted version,
   read in full, 2026-09-30). Verbatim main-text passage:
   > "Fig. 4E shows the external quantum efficiency (EQE) of the certified
   > tandem cell. Under AM1.5G-equivalent illumination conditions, the
   > photogenerated current density Jph in the perovskite and silicon subcells
   > were 19.41 mA cm⁻² and 20.18 mA cm⁻², respectively, which agreed with the
   > measured JSC of 19.23 mA cm⁻². The tandem solar cell exhibited a non-ideal
   > current mismatch of 0.77 mA cm⁻², and although the perovskite cell sets the
   > tandem shunt resistance, the cell reached an FF of 79.5%. The cumulative
   > photogenerated current density and loss caused by reflection were 39.59 mA
   > cm⁻² and 2.57 mA cm⁻², respectively."
   Fig. 4 caption (E): "External quantum efficiency (EQE) and 1-reflection of
   the certified tandem cell measured in-house. The AM1.5G-equivalent current
   densities are given in the legend in mA cm⁻²."

## A.4 Re-digitization protocol (to run when images land)

For each of Fig. 4E and Fig. S32, for each required branch:
1. Crop to plot area + legend region recorded in provenance (Objective A.2).
2. Axis calibration from ≥4 tick marks per axis; record pixel uncertainty.
3. Method 1 (manual points) and Method 2 (color segmentation) and Method 3
   (independent grayscale trace) — three independent calibrations where
   practical; otherwise three methods on one calibration, recorded.
4. Integrate 300–1250 nm (project window; V1.5/V1.6 convention) on the
   recovered spectrum; report 300–1100 / 300–1200 as sensitivity.
5. Shift sensitivity (−25…+25 nm) and window sensitivity (V1.6 tests) re-run
   with the recovered spectrum.
6. Pixel-uncertainty band (V1.6: ~16.61–17.61 for S32) re-computed from the
   new calibration's pixel uncertainty.
7. Compare each independent result against 19.41 and 20.18. **No vertical
   scaling or offset** (guard enforced in code).
