============================================================
1. WHAT APMT IS
============================================================

APMT is our proposed improved perovskite/silicon tandem photovoltaic architecture.

The project started as a Python simulation and evolved into a research-grade experimental-data-ready framework.

Scientific goal:
Determine whether the APMT architecture produces a real, attributable improvement over a conventional perovskite/silicon tandem under controlled conditions.

Correct scientific order:
1) establish a defensible conventional baseline,
2) constrain the baseline with real measurements,
3) freeze the baseline,
4) predict APMT effect,
5) fabricate matched control and APMT devices when justified,
6) test them under comparable conditions,
7) attribute any observed difference only after ruling out ordinary measurement/process differences.

Never reverse this order merely to obtain a nice-looking efficiency number.
